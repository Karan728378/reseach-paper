## 2. Forensic Resolution of the "105 / 42 / 53" Claim

The previous report claimed:
- 105 successful runs
- 42 quota failures
- 53 not executed runs
*(Total planned: 4 variants × 5 tasks × 10 reps = 200 runs)*

### Independent Verification Verdict: **CONFIRMED, WITH CRITICAL SAMPLING BIAS**

Our independent audit of `main_experiment/full-summary.csv` and `main_experiment/agent-history/*.json` definitively verified these numbers and uncovered their exact operational origin:

1. **Scheduled Sequence & Truncation:**
   - The runner scheduled runs with `scheduled_run_index` from 1 to 200.
   - Runs 1 through 105 executed sequentially between `2026-10-11T07:37:57` and `2026-10-11T08:29:58` UTC.
   - Every single run from 1 to 105 succeeded (`success = True`, 100% success rate during this window).
   
2. **Quota Exhaustion Cascade:**
   - At run 106 (`2026-10-11T08:30:08` UTC), Google Gemini API quota was completely exhausted.
   - The exact error recorded in `agent-history/cc4954f0-b3db-4388-bf6a-633853240ddf.json`:
     ```json
     "error": "429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED. Quota exceeded for metric: generativelanguage.googleapis.com/generate_content_free_tier_requests, limit: 500, model: gemini-3.5-flash-lite. Please retry in 15h29m58s."
     ```
   - The runner did **not** halt upon encountering quota exhaustion. Instead, it launched 42 consecutive runs (indices 106 to 147), each running for ~8–10 seconds, retrying 6 times, and exiting after exactly 7 failed steps.
   - At run 147 (`2026-10-11T08:36:27` UTC), execution was manually or automatically halted.

3. **Unexecuted Truncation:**
   - Scheduled run indices 148 through 200 (exactly 53 runs) were never launched.

### Fatal Statistical Consequence: Severe Non-Random Missingness
Because runs were executed in block/scheduled order and truncated deterministically when the daily 500-request quota ran out:
- **Missingness is MNAR (Missing Not At Random) / right-truncated by time.**
- The surviving 105 successful runs do **not** form a balanced or random sample:
  - `v0-baseline`: 30 successes
  - `v1-jsonld`: 23 successes
  - `v2-aria`: 26 successes
  - `v3-data-attrs`: 26 successes
  - Cell counts ($variant \times task$) range from 2 to 8 (e.g., `v1-jsonld | T4` has only 2 completed runs, while `v1-jsonld | T1` has 8).
- Calling the 105 runs a "random sample" or computing omnibus statistics without accounting for cell imbalance is scientifically invalid.

## 3. Discrepancy & Bug Report

1. **Blank `error_message` in Summary CSV:**
   In `results/main_experiment/full-summary.csv`, all 42 failed runs have `error_message = ""` (empty string), even though their raw `agent-history` files contain unambiguous HTTP 429 quota exhaustion errors. This was caused by an extraction flaw in `agent-runner.py`'s `extract_fatal_quota_error()`.
2. **Orphan Raw Run `ec33d1c9`:**
   `results/main_experiment/raw/ec33d1c9-6a3f-42a9-bb20-1e5f86641e78.jsonl` exists in the raw directory but is absent from `full-summary.csv`. Forensic inspection shows it was an interrupted run from 2026-10-11T07:37:35 UTC prior to scheduled run index 1.
3. **Unconditional 0.25 Partial Score on T4 Failures:**
   In `verifier.py:479`, `score += 0.25 # checkout reached` is awarded unconditionally. As a result, all 9 failed T4 runs received a partial score of `0.25` despite zero successful agent actions.
